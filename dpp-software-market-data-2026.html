DPP software market check 2026: 23 vendors on pricing transparency and after-sale coverage

By Elvis Krastins · Published 2026-09-25 · Data checked 2026-09-24 to 2026-09-25

Key numbers (checked 24-25 September 2026): I looked at 23 vendors that sell Digital Product Passport (DPP), registration or warranty software. 14 of 23 publish their pricing; the rest sell through a demo or proposal. 17 offer a DPP. Of those 17, only 3 also show an after-sale owner experience such as registration, ownership or warranty (Veribl, Layerise, Arianee), and 1 of those 3 publishes pricing. The market is still split into compliance tools on one side and registration or warranty tools on the other.

How this was checked

For each vendor I read its public product, pricing and DPP pages between 24 and 25 September 2026 and recorded three things:

  1. DPP: does the vendor offer an EU Digital Product Passport (including battery passports)?
  2. Owner experience: does it show anything for the buyer after purchase: product registration, ownership records, warranty activation or claims? "Partial" means it is implied but not clearly described.
  3. Published pricing: can you see a price or plan without booking a call? This records transparency, not cost.

"No" means I could not find it on public pages. Vendors change their sites, so treat this as a snapshot. Detailed scoring for each segment is in the DPP, battery passport, textile DPP and registration and warranty comparisons.

What stands out

  • Pricing: 14 of 23 vendors (61%) publish pricing. The self-serve DPP tools (dpp.gs, WIARA, MyProductPassport, PassportCraft, DPP Hero, Seamdex) mostly do. Enterprise traceability and automotive vendors mostly don't.
  • Split market: 6 of the 23 vendors do registration or warranty without a DPP, and 14 of the 17 DPP vendors show no clear owner experience. This compares public feature pages; it does not show how many systems or QR codes brands actually deploy.
  • Batteries first: battery passport vendors are the most specialised group because the 18 February 2027 deadline under Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 is the first hard DPP date.

FAQ

How many DPP software vendors publish their pricing?

In this check, 10 of the 17 DPP vendors publish pricing.

Which DPP tools also handle product registration and warranty?

Veribl, Layerise and Arianee show both a DPP and an owner experience on their public pages. EON partly does. Of these, Veribl is the one with published pricing.
