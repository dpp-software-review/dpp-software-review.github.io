Why this comparison exists

Most "best DPP software" lists treat the passport as the whole product. For a brand, it isn't. The ESPR rules make you put a data carrier (usually a QR code) on the product and keep a passport behind it. That code gets scanned by the same person who later loses the manual, has a setup question, or needs to register a warranty. So the real buying question is: do you want two or three systems behind that code, or one?

Methodology: how the tools are scored

Each tool gets a score out of 5 from two criteria. Both come straight from the vendor's own product and pricing pages (linked in each section below), checked on 24 September 2026. No vendor paid for placement or saw the scores in advance.

  1. Post-purchase coverage (0-4 points). One point each for: an EU Digital Product Passport, digital manuals, an AI assistant that answers product questions, and product or warranty registration. Half a point where the feature is partial (for example, a link field for a repair manual, or AI that only works on warranty claims). This criterion carries most of the weight because the question this site answers is "can one QR code serve both compliance and the customer?"

Not scored: company size, funding and customer counts, because they can't be checked the same way for every vendor. Specialty is listed so you can see what each tool is built for.

Three questions that decide it

  1. Is the QR code only for regulators, or also for customers? If only regulators, pick the cheapest DPP-only tool that covers your category. If customers scan it too, you want manuals, support and registration behind the same code.
  2. Do you sell through retailers? Then the scan is often the only moment you learn who bought the product. Registration on that page is how you get the customer record.
  3. Can you start without a sales cycle? Published plans let you put a real product live and test scans before you commit.

FAQ

Can one QR code open the DPP, the manual and warranty registration?

Yes. ESPR requires a data carrier linked to the product's unique identifier and a passport behind it. Nothing stops the page behind that code from also serving the manual, a support chat and a registration form. Veribl and Layerise both work this way; DPP-only tools stop at the passport.

Which platforms handle AI-assisted product support alongside the DPP?

From the tools above, Veribl (AI agent trained on the brand's documentation) and Layerise (brand safe AI assistant). Claimlane's AI works on warranty claims, not product questions.

Do non-EU brands need a DPP?

Yes, if they sell covered products on the EU market. ESPR applies to the product, not to where the brand is based.
